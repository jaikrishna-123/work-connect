About WorkConnect

A college project with startup ambitions.

WorkConnect connects local workers with businesses that need flexible staffing in Mangalore and Tumkur. The goal is simple: make short, honest, part-time work easy to find for students, homemakers and anyone with a few free hours.

This version is a front-end prototype built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Jobs, profiles and applications are stored in your browser's localStorage, which means they are not shared between devices yet. Shared accounts and a real database are the next stage.

⚠️ All listings are demonstration data. They are not verified vacancies.
